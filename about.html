What The Detox Solution is, and how it works.

A nicotine vending company serving adult residential treatment and recovery facilities in the United States. We own the machines, license them, install them, stock them and service them, at no cost to the facility.

Who we are

The Detox Solution is a company-operated vending service, not an individual owner-operator route. Facility partnerships, installation, restocking, servicing and regulatory compliance are handled by our operations team. Every enquiry, from a facility director, a prospective partner or a resident with a refund question, goes to one place: info@thedetoxsolution.com.

We work only with adult-only residential facilities where nicotine is permitted. We do not place machines in facilities that serve minors, and we do not approach nicotine-free programs. Those are hard limits, not preferences.

A facility gets a fully managed machine, pays nothing, and does nothing beyond telling us where to put it.

What we actually do

Adults in residential treatment who already use nicotine generally have two options: leave campus to buy it, or go without. The first creates a supervision problem for the facility. We provide a third option: a controlled, locked machine on site that removes the reason to leave.

  • The machine. Purchased, owned, delivered and installed by us. Install takes under 45 minutes and needs nothing from facility staff.
  • Restocking. A local manager monitors inventory remotely and comes on site to refill it. Nothing ships to the facility, and no staff member ever handles product.
  • Service and maintenance. Hardware faults and repairs are ours.
  • Compliance. State licensing, sales tax and reporting sit with us. The facility carries none of it.
  • Refunds. If a purchase fails, the machine refunds the resident automatically. Staff never handle a dispute.

Why it costs the facility nothing

The program is funded by resident purchases. That is the whole model. The facility is never billed, never fronts money and never signs a financial commitment.

How the machine is controlled

Every unit is cashless and locked. It runs on its own cellular connection and never touches the facility’s network, so there is no IT review and no credentials to issue. The cabinet is locking steel. The facility chooses the placement, sets the hours and house rules, and can have it switched off at any time. Full hardware specifications are on the machine page.

Leaving

There is no long-term obligation. If the service isn’t right for a facility, we remove the machine at no cost. We would rather make leaving easy than have a machine somewhere it isn’t wanted.

Common questions.

Who owns and operates The Detox Solution?

The Detox Solution is company-operated. Facility partnerships and day-to-day operations are managed by our operations team, reachable at info@thedetoxsolution.com.

Is this a franchise or a route I can buy into?

No. We don’t sell routes, territories or franchises, and machines are not sold to third parties. We own and operate every machine we place.

What does the facility have to do?

Choose where the machine goes. That is the entire ask. No restocking, no deliveries to accept, no keys, no paperwork and no staff training.

Which facilities qualify?

Adult-only residential treatment, detox, rehabilitation and behavioral health facilities where nicotine is permitted. If a program is nicotine-free or serves minors, we are not a fit and we will say so on the first call.

How do I reach someone?

Email info@thedetoxsolution.com, call (216) 930-2575, or send us your details on the contact page.
