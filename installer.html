02 — Step by Step

Complete install checklist

1

Accept the freight delivery

Machine arrives via liftgate truck on a pallet in a crate. Inspect for damage before signing. Photograph any damage before you sign anything.

2

Uncrate the machine

Remove crate panels from the top down. The machine has caster wheels on the base. Do not lift it — roll it.

3

Roll to position

The facility contact will show you where the machine is going. Roll slowly. Take corners carefully.

4

Lock the caster wheels

Lock all four wheels once in final position. Show the facility contact where the locks are.

5

Tighten aisle screws

Open the front door with the key provided. Tighten all aisle screws with your Phillips head screwdriver before stocking.

6

Debug all coils

Spin every coil manually to confirm it rotates freely. If a coil does not spin, call us before proceeding.

7

Install the QR code

Apply the QR code sticker to the designated spot on the machine exterior as shown in the video.

8

Plug in power and wireless kit

Plug the power cord into the nearest outlet. Plug the ethernet cable into the wireless kit. No WiFi needed — it connects automatically over cellular.

9

Confirm boot up

The touchscreen should light up within 60 seconds. If it does not turn on within 2 minutes, call us before doing anything else.

10

Stock the machine

Load products matching the aisle labels. Use your 2 trash bags for all packing materials. Leave no packaging in the facility.

11

Run a live transaction

Purchase one item to confirm the card reader, coil, and dispensing all work. We will reimburse this charge.
