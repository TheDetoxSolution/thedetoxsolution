What does it cost us?

Nothing. There is no lease, fee, invoice or minimum. We buy the machine, the product and the service. Resident purchases fund the program.

Why don’t you share revenue with the facility?

On purpose. A cut of nicotine sales would put a treatment program in the position of profiting from resident nicotine use, and that is a line your board would have to defend. Keeping money out of the relationship removes the question.

Who restocks and services it?

Our local manager, on site. Your staff never loads product, opens the machine, accepts a delivery or handles a refund.

What does it sell?

Nicotine products we agree with you before install, within state law. You can change the list, the hours and the house rules at any time.

Does it need our Wi-Fi or IT?

No. It runs on its own cellular connection and needs only a standard outlet. There is nothing for IT to review.

How fast can it be live?

Usually about two weeks for the state licence, depending on the state. The install itself takes under 45 minutes.

What if it isn’t working for us?

Tell us and we remove it at no cost. There is no contract to get out of.
