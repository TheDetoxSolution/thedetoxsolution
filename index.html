Questions, Answered
The things facilities ask first.
How is this really free? What is the catch?

There is no catch. We own the machine, cover the installation, and purchase the product. Residents buy from the machine directly, and that is what sustains the program. Your facility never pays anything, never fronts money, and is never billed. There is also no revenue share — we keep it clean on purpose, so nicotine sales never become a line item or a compliance question on your side.

What does our staff actually have to do?

Nothing. This is the question we get most, and the answer genuinely is nothing. Our local manager restocks the machine on site, services it, and handles refunds and compliance. Your staff does not load product, accept deliveries, hold keys, or train on anything. Your only role is telling us where the machine should go.

Is this safe for our residents?

We place machines only in adult-only facilities where nicotine is permitted. Where we are a fit, the machine is the controlled option — the alternative is usually residents leaving campus to buy nicotine. It is 21+ ID-verified, cashless, locked, and you decide the placement, the hours, and the house rules. You can have it switched off at any time.

Is this licensed and compliant?

Yes. We hold the required tobacco and OTP vending licensing in the states where we operate and manage all compliance, tax, and reporting ourselves. Your facility takes on no regulatory burden.

What happens if a resident's purchase does not dispense?

The machine detects it and issues a refund automatically, with no staff involvement. If a product is ever defective, the resident scans the QR code on the machine, submits a short form, and is refunded within 72 hours — handled entirely on our end.

Is the machine discreet? Our environment matters to us.

It is a clean, modern, self-contained unit, and we place it wherever works best for your facility. It is cashless and quiet, and we work with you on placement so it fits naturally into your environment.

What if we want it removed?

Then we remove it, at no cost. There is no long-term obligation. If the service is not right for your facility, we make leaving as easy as starting.

How long does it take to get started?

Once we have spoken and aligned on placement, we coordinate delivery and installation and have the machine running shortly after. The install itself takes under 45 minutes. We move at a pace that works for your facility.
