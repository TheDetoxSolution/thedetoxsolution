Who services the machines?

We do. A local manager restocks and services each machine on site. Hardware faults, repairs and refunds are ours. Your staff never opens the machine.

What if a program doesn’t want it anymore?

Tell us and we remove it at no cost. There is no term and nothing to get out of, at any single site or across the network.

How fast can a site go live?

Usually about two weeks for the state tobacco licence, depending on the state. The install takes under 45 minutes.

Who holds the licence and pays the tax?

We do. The state tobacco licence is in our name, and we collect and remit sales tax. Your organization carries none of it.

Can each program set its own rules?

Yes. Each site chooses the placement, the hours, the house rules and the product list, within state law, and can change them at any time.

Why no revenue share?

So that no program ever profits from resident nicotine use. It keeps the relationship clean for your compliance team and your board.

Does it connect to our systems?

No. It runs on its own cellular line, needs one standard outlet, and never touches your network.
